Owner-bound companion slots on a hero or tower, baked into a CompanionDef (template custom data). Each slot keeps one child alive: a ground escort (an ordinary soldier that blocks and follows the owner's move or rally orders) or a flying follower (keeps near the owner, never blocks, attacks from where it is). A fallen child is replaced after refillDelay, counted from its death. Child damage earns the owner experience.
