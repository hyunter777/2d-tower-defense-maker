Extra rules for the enemies released by deathSummon (successors), baked with WithDeathSuccession(int, float, SuccessorBountyPolicy, bool, bool): a shared chain budget (successors of successors), a rise time and the bounty rule. A lineage never pays its bounty twice and leaks only once.
