Class UnitPresets

Fills enemy, hero and soldier assets from ratios to a baseline (the project's median enemy and soldier, or built-in defaults). The ratios stay inside the bands of the balance notes: bounty density ×0.5–1.8 of the baseline, defenses in the low (0–0.2), medium (0.2–0.5) and strong (0.5–0.7) bands, hero experience steps ×1.1–1.6 after a first jump of ×2.5–3.5. They are a starting point; tune the result against your own towers.
